      How to play RaDrop

      1 Be the first to empty your hand

      Everyone gets 7 cards. Take turns playing them onto the pile. Whoever plays their last card wins. There are no points; just play again.

      →

      2 Match the color or the number

      Your card must match the top card of the pile by color or by number / symbol. Wild cards go on anything.

      →

      3 Can’t play? Draw one

      Press Draw or tap the deck. If the new card fits you can play it straight away, or press Pass. If it doesn’t fit, your turn ends.

      →

      4 Drop several cards at once

      Holding several cards with the same number? Drop them all at once, whatever their colors, as long as one of them matches. Then you choose which of those colors carries on.

      → + + →

      In the game: tap a 2 and all your 2s are selected. Tap one to leave it out, press Drop, then pick the color.

      Action cards combine too: several of the same symbol (Skips, Reverses or +2s, any colors), or any mix of Skip, Reverse and +2 in one color. Each kind works: the Reverse flips play, the Skip jumps the next player, and every +2 counts against the player after that. When you tap a card you can drop the whole set or play just that one.

      → + → play flips,
      next player skipped
      → + → play flips, then the
      next player faces +2
      + → one player skipped,
      you pick Amber or Violet

      While a +2 is coming at you, only +2s can be combined (see 6).

      5 Action cards

      6 A +2 coming at you? Answer it

      Drop a +2 of any color and the penalty grows and moves on to the next player. Or bounce it back to whoever played it with a Reverse of the same color. Can’t answer? You draw the whole stack.

      →

      Got two +2s? Drop them together, whatever their colors. Both count: the next player faces +4, and you pick the color. This works when you answer a +2 too.

      + → next player faces +4,
      you pick Ruby or Jade

      A Wild +4 can only be answered with another Wild +4.

      7 Call “Last card!”

      When you’re about to be down to one card, press Last card! before or right after you play. If someone presses Catch! on you first, you draw 2.

      Last card! or they Catch! = you draw 2

      8 30 seconds per turn

      A countdown shows how long you have. It turns red for the last 5 seconds. At zero, autopilot plays for you: it drops your best card, or draws.

      30s → 3s → autopilot plays

      Keyboard: D draw · P pass · L last card · Esc cancel · Enter chat · 1–4 pick a color
